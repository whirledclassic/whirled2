# The Room — a pitch for people who already built worlds

Working title: **Whirled 2**. The name can change. The shape cannot.

This is not a request to license Mickey, Call of Duty, or anyone’s catalog. It is a request to look at a product idea that already worked once, died with Flash, and is cheap enough to try again in a normal browser.

Repo: https://github.com/whirledclassic/whirled2  
Maintainer: Josh · josh.awe99@gmail.com  
Status: public repo open. First ship is one click-to-walk room ([issue #1](https://github.com/whirledclassic/whirled2/issues/1)). No live URL yet. That gap is the honest part.

## What Whirled was

Three Rings (Puzzle Pirates) shipped **Whirled** in 2008: a browser virtual world where *players* made the catalog.

- Your room was the profile. Friends walked into it.
- Avatars, furniture, pets, tunes, backdrops, and whole Flash games were user-made and sold in a shop.
- Time currency (Coins) and cash currency (Bars). Creators cashed out Bling. Split was roughly a third to the maker, a third to the house, a third to the affiliate.
- No download. Flash applet + HTML sidebar. Open the tab, you were in.

It was MySpace you could *walk through*, plus a toybox, plus a tiny game platform. That combination is still rare.

Official world closed 8 April 2017. Community mirrors and a public Java source tree kept the idea from vanishing. We are not those mirrors. We are a **new** client.

## Why the idea is stronger in 2026 than in 2010

In 2010 the CEO said the quiet part: about **$5M into Whirled, about $300K back**. Flash was the floor. A San Francisco studio was the cost. The idea was not the failure. The stack was.

Now:

| Then | Now |
| --- | --- |
| Flash Player | Chrome / Safari / a PWA |
| Java cluster + GWT chrome | TypeScript + Pixi, small server later |
| Install nothing *if* you had the plugin | Install nothing, period |
| Shop items that could crash the client | Moderation and allowlists first |
| One company’s catalog | People’s rooms as the social graph |

Fortnite Creative, Roblox, Rec Room, and Horizon already proved UGC worlds print money when the client is everywhere. They also proved something else: most of them want a **download**, a store, a 3D viewport, and a kid-safety stack the size of a bank.

Whirled’s remaining superpower is **thin**. A link. A room. An avatar that walks. A profile that *is* the room. Embeddable on a page the way a YouTube video is embeddable. That is a different product than an island inside someone else’s exe.

## The product, if we get to build the whole thing

**Layer 0 — the room (this quarter)**  
Click-to-walk. Floor, walls, one body. Ugly is allowed. Public HTTPS when it exists.

**Layer 1 — the person**  
MySpace-grade profile that is not a wall of fields. The room *is* the profile. Banner, song, featured creations, friends who can actually stand in the doorway.

**Layer 2 — the stuff**  
User furniture and avatars we own the rights to. No ripped 2010 shop. Credit always. Pay creators if the live world makes money.

**Layer 3 — the games**  
Small games that live *in* a room, the way Whirled games sat on a table. Not a competing Fortnite. A toy you drop in someone’s house.

**Layer 4 — the app skin**  
Same world as a PWA / lightweight native shell. Push when a friend is in your room. Camera-roll costume pieces. Not a second product — the same URL with pockets.

Rename any layer. Keep the verb: **walk into someone’s page.**

## What a studio actually gets from talking to us

Not “buy this unfinished repo.” Three useful shapes:

1. **Advice / a look.** People who shipped Club Penguin, Toontown, Fortnite Creative, Rec Room, IMVU, Second Life already know the trap. Ten minutes of “don’t do X” is worth more than a term sheet.
2. **A thin embed.** If you have IP you want *in a tab* — not only inside Fortnite — a licensed room skin is a different surface than a UEFN island.
3. **Hiring / contracting later.** When there is a URL. Not before.

We will rename the public product if a rights holder asks. Classic source stays in `classic/` as preservation. The new room does not ship Three Rings art.

## What we are not asking for

- A $5 million check. That number already failed once. See [PUBLIC.md](PUBLIC.md).
- Disney or Activision to “host Whirled.” They host their own worlds, or they host them inside Epic.
- Anyone to pretend a walkable rectangle is a shipped MMO.

## One paragraph you can forward

Whirled was a 2008 browser world where your homepage was a room people walked into, and the catalog was made by the players. Flash killed the official site in 2017. The idea — a user-generated social world that is just a URL — is sitting on the table again because the browser can finally do it without a plugin. We are building that room in the open (TypeScript / Pixi). The name can change. We want people who have already shipped a world to tell us what not to repeat, and, when there is a public URL, whether a thin embed belongs next to the heavy platforms.
