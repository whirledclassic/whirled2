What this is

Whirled 2 is a new browser world in the spirit of Whirled (2007–2017): your room, walk-around avatars, user-made stuff. No Flash. No ripped old shop items.

We need people across the whole project — not only illustrators. Pick every role you can actually do. Send links to work you already shipped.

There is a paid opportunity

Yes, you can get paid. Apply, show the work, tell us what you want to charge. If we accept you and we like what you bring, we pay for that work.

Acceptance is not automatic. It depends on the application, the links you send, and whether the first job is a fit. If we say yes, we pay for that job.

Want to stay on after the first job? Say so. Right now that still looks like one payment per drop (a walk cycle, a room, a feature, a month of Discord). We stay in touch and do the next payment when the next drop is agreed.

You can also apply as volunteer / credit-only if money is not the point. Credit either way.
