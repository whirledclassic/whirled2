Whirled was a 2008 browser world from Three Rings. Your homepage was a room. Avatars walked. People made the catalog. Flash died, the official world closed in 2017, and whirled.club kept a revival client alive as a download.

This remake is a new public room in a normal tab: TypeScript + PixiJS. Same verbs. New chrome. No Flash, no ripped shop, no claim on their domain.
