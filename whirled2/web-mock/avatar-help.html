How avatars work

Your Stuff shelf is what you already own. Wear an avatar, then click the loft floor to walk.

Upload is locked

Not every account can upload. You need a membership, then creator. Developers can always upload. Join / Buy on Club cannot grant membership while payments are off. A moderator or developer grants it.

If you are a creator

  1. Stuff → Avatars
  2. Use idle + walk PNG frames, or Whirl. Classic SWF is experimental.
  3. Keep files under 10 MB. Use art you own.
  4. Wear it, then test walk in Rooms.

If you are not a creator

You can still Wear avatars you already have. Open Me → Club and apply. Staff grant the real flags.
